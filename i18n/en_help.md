## `LabelTIntroductionWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TIntroductionWHelp`_

In this game you will find yourself in a beautiful fantasy garden inhabited by fairy creatures. The garden was decorated with beautiful patterns made of little twigs. One day a terrible hurricane swept down and destroyed them, having left little of what used to be the garden's main attraction. Your mission is to connect twigs and restore the original patterns.

Twigs pass by at the bottom part of the screen, slowly accumulating. The initial twig is already on the playing field, you should connect to it a matching twig and continue connecting twigs with each other until you reach the top of the screen. All twigs should be connected, otherwise you'll never reach the top. If some twig has an open joint, you won't move up, because it is necessary that all elements on the playing field be connected with each other. It is also important to watch the conveyer, because when it overfills, you lose.

In some levels there is an object on the playing field, which you should entwine into your pattern with the help of twigs. There are also flying objects which hinder you from placing twigs, so you should wait until they pass by. Magic tools will help you during the game - you may use the Clipper to cut unnecessary elements or to insert twigs of the desired shape, and the Hammer to smash twigs at the bottom of the screen to prevent the conveyer from overfilling.

## `LabelTGettingStartedWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TGettingStartedWHelp`_

When you start the game, you are suggested to select a starting bonus and the level of difficulty.

You may choose between the following starting bonuses: the Hammer, the Amphora and Additional Score. Having chosen one of the starting bonuses, you will get more magic tools or additional points.

After you selected a starting bonus, choose the level of difficulty of the game. You can play in the following game modes:

No Lose - you never lose, but you don't score points

Easy - twigs move slowly and you score some points

Normal  - twigs appear at a normal speed and you earn points for your actions

Hard  - twigs appear at a high speed and you can get a higher score

## `LabelTPlayingFieldWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TPlayingFieldWHelp`_

The playing field consists of three parts. In the center there is the field for restoring a pattern. The initial twig is already given, you should connect to it another twig and continue until you reach the top.

At the bottom part of the screen there is the conveyer which gradually fills with twigs of various shapes. When there is no empty space left, you lose the game. You must use twigs for restoring the original pattern.

On the right there is the information panel and the magic tools. The information panel displays your score, the current level and the "Menu" button.

The magic tools, the Amphora and the Hammer, can help you in the course of the game a lot. You may use the Amphora to to create twigs of the desired shape or to cut the unnecessary elements, and the Hammer to smash twigs in the conveyer and on the playing field. Below the instruments there is the Drop Section, it may be used for throwing out the unnecessary twigs.

## `LabelTHowToPlayWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `THowToPlayWHelp`_

The basic rules of the game are as follows:

- Left-click the twig you want to use.

- Rotate the twig if it is necessary by clicking the right mouse      button.

- Attach the twig to the one on the playing field by left-clicking     the mouse.

- Connect all the twigs on the playing field with each other.

- Connect the group of twigs to the top of the tree.

## `LabelTArrangingTwigsWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TArrangingTwigsWHelp`_

Twigs are delivered by the conveyer at the bottom of the screen. The goal of the game is to connect the twigs with each other on the playing field, so as to reach the top of the screen. The twigs in the conveyer are slowly accumulating, so you should keep an eye on it not to let it overfill, otherwise the game will end. Along with common twigs, there are two special types of twigs: Mystery Twigs - you don't know what shape they have until you take them, and Lucky Twigs - when you use them you get certain bonuses. On the playing field you can also see Transparent Twigs - you should cover them with twigs of the same shape.

Twigs can have various shapes, and all of them have different number of joints. When a twig has an open joint, there is light coming from it to show that it is possible to connect another twig to it. Lower joints shouldn't be left open, because moving upwards is only possible when all twigs are connected with each other. Open joints preventing you from moving up are indicated with green light. If it is possible to connect one twig to another, you'll see a green arrow, and if it's impossible - you'll see a red cross.

## `LabelTMagicToolsWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TMagicToolsWHelp`_

Magic Tools can help you to pass the game levels. The number in the top right-hand corner of the tool icon shows how many tools of this type you have. Some bonuses may increase the number of uses of a tool.

Hammer. This tool can be used for smashing twigs in the conveyer and on the playing field (except the initial ones). Use the hammer to smash the twigs that don't fit in your pattern to free up space for the new ones.

USING THE HAMMER: Click on the hammer icon to select it. Place the hammer on a desired twig and click the left mouse button to smash it. You can smash several twigs in a row. If you want to drop the hammer, just click the right mouse button.

Amphora. You may want to use this tool in two cases: when you cannot find a twig of the desired shape and when you need to cut the unnecessary elements.

USING THE AMPHORA: Click on the icon to select this tool. To create a missing twig place the amphora in the desired position on the playing field and click the left mouse button. To cut the unnecessary elements place the amphora on the desired twig and click it. Click the right mouse button to drop the amphora.

## `LabelTSkillsAndExperienceWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TSkillsAndExperienceWHelp`_

At the end of each level you are given some points of Experience. The number of experience points depends on the number of twigs which you used in restoring patterns in the previous level, and on the number of twig combinations you've made (one point for each combination, and two points if all combinations were made).

You can allot your experience points among the five Skills which are shown in the left part of the dialog window:

Time Control - allows you not to lose for some time even when the conveyer has overfilled with gems.

Drop Section Accelerator - makes the Drop Section work faster.

Good Luck - brings more Lucky Twigs (the ones that have bonuses).

Amphora - creates twigs of the desired shape and cuts the unnecessary elements.

Hammer -  smashes the unnecessary twigs in the conveyer and on the playing field.

## `LabelTCombinationsWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TCombinationsWHelp`_

If you make special twig combinations, while restoring a pattern, you may get various bonuses. Making combinations is not difficult: you should connect twigs the way it is shown on the picture in the right-hand part of the screen. When you make a combination, the picture disappears, and a new one appears for you to make with the help of twigs.

## `LabelTScoringPointsWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TScoringPointsWHelp`_

You score points for each twig on the field. The number of points for each twig depends on the number of joints it has. It means that twigs with a larger number of joints bring more points. Besides, some bonuses obtained for making twig combinations and using bonus twigs also bring additional points.

During the game you also earn Experience Points. The number of experience points depends on the number of twigs which you used in restoring patterns, and on the number of twig combinations you've made (one point for each combination, and two points if all combinations were made). So, try to use gems with a larger number of joints and make twig combinations - and you'll be able to improve your skills.

## `LabelTShortcutsWHelp`
_widget `TLabLabel` · size `495x470` · form `TShortcutsWHelp`_

Space bar - pick up the first twig in the conveyer. If the conveyer is empty, a new twig will be generated.

'1', '2'... '9' -  pick up the twig with the corresponding number in the conveyer.

F3 - take (place back) the Hammer.

F4 - take (place back) the Amphora.

Del - throw away the selected twig.

Esc - view the menu.
